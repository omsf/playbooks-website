The section contains various documents to help you understand different concepts and reasoning behind our recommendations for working with AWS.
This is based on the [Diàtaxis definition for explanation](https://diataxis.fr/understanding/), which states that:

> Explanation is a discursive treatment of a subject, that permits reflection. Explanation is **understanding-oriented.**

The documents in this section will explain why we make certain choices, provide guidance on how to make decisions, and provide more information to help you understand some of the concepts behind our approaches to working with AWS.
In general, this will expand on the specific tasks from the [How-to section](../how-tos/) and provide more context what is happening in those step-by-step guides.
