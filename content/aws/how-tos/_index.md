This section will contain various how-to guides to help you through different tasks tthat you may encounter while working with AWS.
According to the [Diàtaxis definition](https://diataxis.fr/how-to-guides/), a how-to guide is:

> How-to guides are **directions** that guide the reader through a problem or towards a result. How-to guides are **goal-oriented.**

These guides aim to provide you with practical, step-by-step instructions to accomplish specific tasks in AWS.
This isn't the place to discuss theory or concepts in depth, but rather to focus on actionable steps that you can follow to achieve your objectives.
For more conceptual discussion, see the [Explanation section](../explanation/).
