## Introduction

The AWS playbook is a highly-opinionated guide for managing AWS accounts and resources.
These are the practices that have been working for OMSF; these may not be best for large-scale organizations, but they are what have been working for us.
The goal here is to have this be a resource that makes it easier for new projects to align their AWS setups with what we recommend.
